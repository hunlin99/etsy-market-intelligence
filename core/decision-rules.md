# Provisional Phase 1 decision rules

These are conservative prototype rules, not calibrated commercial predictions. The program evaluates structured analyst judgments; it does not discover or verify them from raw prose.

Five prerequisite gates cover compliance, customer problem, production feasibility, critical risk, and economics. Known gate values require evidence. Unknown is distinct from failure.

Apply precedence in this order:

1. **Reject:** an evidence-backed gate is fail, unacceptable, or nonviable. Stop or redesign; do not use research volume to override a hard failure.
2. **Proceed:** all gates are favorable; there are no critical unknowns, unavailable sources, or conflicts; paid-test and economics evidence exists; the validated target exactly matches the requested target; decisive evidence has at least medium directness, reliability, and freshness. This approves only the stated bounded action.
3. **Research More:** the analyst specifies a bounded, decision-relevant research question.
4. **Monitor:** the analyst supplies an explicit revisit trigger. This does not schedule anything.
5. **Test First:** there is qualifying buyer-problem or paid-test evidence but not enough to approve the target investment. Design a smaller affordable experiment.
6. **Explore:** no qualifying buyer signal supports an investment decision yet.

Do not count reviews, rankings, badges, listing counts, or trends as paid demand. Do not infer precise sales or revenue from them. Do not use a numerical opportunity score.

Prototype confidence is capped at medium for Proceed and low otherwise (unknown without references). It is intentionally not an average of evidence quality. More nuanced claim-level confidence and human calibration remain future work.

Read the stored decision, its supporting and contrary IDs, counter-case, unknowns, change conditions, and bounded action into every report. Review semantic alignment between action and verdict manually.
