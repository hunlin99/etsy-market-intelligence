# Critical review

Before any decision, state at least one strong, reasonable counter-case. For example: a free workaround may already solve the buyer's problem, or a paid pilot may depend on an audience the seller cannot reproduce.

Record alternative explanations, contrary evidence, sample selection bias, freshness, and source dependence. If two sources disagree, preserve both and describe the conflict. Unresolved conflicts block Proceed in this prototype.

Test the claim most likely to change the decision. Do not challenge for its own sake, flatter user preferences, or treat an absence of evidence as proof that demand does not exist.

Every next action needs an observable criterion and an effort or cost bound. A response must explain what evidence would change the decision. Human review must confirm these criteria are meaningful for the user's actual constraints.
