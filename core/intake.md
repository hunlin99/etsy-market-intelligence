# Intake

Identify the question, product, marketplace scope, geography, depth, and specific next investment (`learn`, `shortlist`, `prototype`, `launch_mvp`, `expand_catalog`, or `run_ads`). Phase 1 supports quick and standard depth only.

Capture budget, available hours, acceptable loss, and skills in explicit brief assumptions until structured constraints are expanded. If missing, propose a bounded research step and confirm affordability before spending. Do not silently authorize a financial commitment.

Record the actual capability booleans independently. Having a calculator does not imply web access; having uploaded files does not imply seller statistics. Do not claim a tool is available unless the host exposes it.

Ask only questions that could change the next decision. Start with supplied information when reasonable. `user_preference` provides context but is excluded from deterministic verdict selection.
